    04 / Evidence by branch

    Which inputs pushed the score

    The model combines three groups of inputs before its final layers. We re-ran those final layers three more times, each time replacing one group with zeros, and measured how P(fake) changed. If P(fake) drops without a group, that group pushed toward fake; if it rises, the group pushed toward real.
